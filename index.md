I'm an experimental physicist (PhD, Universität Heidelberg) moving into optical system design. This journal documents my progression through Zemax OpticStudio: sequential ray optics, system design and optimization, light source coupling, and illumination optics, building toward a capstone photoluminescence instrument design.

Each post covers one week of hands-on work: the problem, the approach, the result, and what surprised me. Full project files (Zemax `.zmx` files, specs, images) live in the companion [GitHub repository](https://github.com/spark-violet/zemax-learning-journal).

See the [Projects](/projects/) page for a structured index of everything built so far.
